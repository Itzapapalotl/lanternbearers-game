How to play in 4 steps

Easy enough to read aloud. There are no timers, so nobody ever has to hurry.

  1. Meet your party. All four heroes go on every adventure. Each player can take a hero and write their name on its card, or one person can run the whole party.
  2. Tap a hero, then tap a glowing square. That's how you move. Everything is one tap at a time.
  3. Light the braziers and defeat the monsters. Stand next to a brazier to light it. Then attack, or use a special ability. When the room is bright and the monsters are gone, you move on.
  4. Help each other. If a hero is knocked down, a friend standing next to them can help them up. Nobody gets left behind.

Who you play and what you face

Hearts are how much a hero can take. Each special ability can be used once per room.

The four Lanternbearers

Rowan

A brave knight who keeps her friends safe. Shield Wall: +2 Defense, and monsters must hit her instead of friends beside her.

14 hearts

Ranger

A sharp-eyed archer who can hit a target from across the room. Twin Arrows: shoot two arrows in one turn.

10 hearts, long range

Wizard

A clever wizard with sparkly spells. Fireburst: a fireball that always hits every monster around the spot.

8 hearts, ranged

Oren

Lightbringer

A kind healer. Mend heals a friend every turn, and Sunburst heals everyone nearby and nudges monsters back.

11 hearts, healer

The two bosses

Gloamwing

Chapter 1: The Lantern-Eater

A giant shadow moth that gobbles up every bit of light it can find. It's meant to be a real challenge, so bring the whole party. 55 hearts.

Pebblebelly

Chapter 2: The Color Gobbler

A huge, chubby grey dragon with a belly full of stolen rainbow flames. Watch which color it glows next: that's its next move. 46 hearts.

Nibbet's Lantern Shop

After every room you clear, Nibbet sets out three items. Spend the gold you've found on potions, charms and other shiny things for the party bag.

Tips

  • Light the braziers first. Stand next to one and use your action to light it. Bright light helps you hit (+2), and when every brazier is lit the whole party heals a little.
  • Help friends up. A knocked-down hero isn't out. A friend standing next to them can help them back on their feet.
  • Abilities come back each room. Each hero's special ability works once per room, so use it freely. Oren's Mend is free every turn.
  • Chapter 2's colored flames do things. Green light heals 1 heart at the start of your turn, blue slows monsters, red adds damage, and violet boosts Defense.
  • Spend your gold at Nibbet's. It's there to be used. Small items are an easy buy most rooms, and the big ones are worth saving up for.
  • Bag items take two taps. The first tap tells you what the item does, and the second tap uses it. So you can always look first.
  • Gloamwing is meant to be a tough fight. It eats the light of braziers to heal, so relight them fast, keep Oren's Mend going, and spread out a little so its Wing Gust doesn't catch everyone.
  • Want the story read to you? Turn on read-aloud in Settings. It works in Chrome.

Phone and tablet

Lanternbearers plays right in the browser. There's nothing to install, and your progress saves in the browser. It's roomiest on a tablet or laptop, but phones work too.

Want an icon on your home screen?
  • Android, Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone or iPad, Safari: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
